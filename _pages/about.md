I am a PhD student at IST Austria, working under the supervision of Thomas A. Henzinger. My research lies in the intersection of formal verification, statistics, and machine learning, with a particular emphasis on uncertainty quantification. The ultimate goal is to make AI safe and trustworthy when embedded into real world dynamical systems.

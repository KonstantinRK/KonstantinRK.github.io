I am a PhD student at [Institute of Science and Technology Austria](https://ista.ac.at/), working under the supervision of [Thomas A. Henzinger](https://pub.ista.ac.at/~tah/). My research lies in the intersection of formal verification, statistics, and machine learning, with a particular emphasis on uncertainty quantification. The ultimate goal is to make AI safe and trustworthy when deployed in real-world dynamical systems. 


Main Projects
======
My research interests include: 
Runtime Verification, Formal Methods, Stochastic Processes, Non-Asymptotic Statistics, Time Series, Learning Theory, Responsible Computing.
